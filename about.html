About The Stack Times

A newsroom owned by no one but its readers.

The Bay Area is one of the most consequential regions on earth — and one of the worst-covered. As corporate chains hollowed out local newsrooms, the stories that shape daily life here — the council vote, the rent check, the bus that didn't come — lost their watchdogs.

The Stack Times exists to fill that gap. We're an independent, reader-supported newsroom covering the San Francisco Bay Area from the ground up: Oakland, San Francisco, San Jose, and every city in between.

🗞️ Independent

No corporate parent, no political patron, no advertiser calling the shots. Readers fund us; readers own our loyalty.

🔍 On the ground

We go to the meeting, knock on the door, and read the document — not just the press release about it.

🤝 Accountable

When we get something wrong, we correct it fast and in public. Our standards are posted, not assumed.

What we cover

City Hall and county government, housing and homelessness, transit, tech's impact on real life, food, culture, and the neighborhoods that make the Bay Area home. If it affects how people live here, it's our beat.

How we're funded

By readers. The newsletter is free; paid memberships (coming soon) will keep the lights on and the reporting independent. We do not take money from the governments, companies, or campaigns we cover.
